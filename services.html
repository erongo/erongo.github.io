Public Services

These services are not monetized and are made available in a best effort capacity. Some services are geo-restricted to prevent abuse.

radio.erongo.net | NAMIBIA

Code Radio

This service streams lofi music 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. It supports command line, desktop, and web clients. In order to prevent excessive bandwidth usage, it limits the number of simultaneous connections.

headlines.erongo.net | GLOBAL

Daily News Headlines

Aggregated news headlines from Namibia as they happen. Headlines link back to original sources. Please support sources you trust so Namibian media can remain strong and independent.
